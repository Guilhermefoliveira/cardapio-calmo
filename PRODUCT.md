# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

- Customers at the counter or at a table in one of the two Calmô shops in Florianópolis (Beira Mar and Centro), phone in one hand, deciding what to order in under a minute. Mobile is the primary device.
- People arriving from Instagram (@querocalmo) or a shared link who want to see what Calmô serves before visiting or ordering through iFood.
- Tourists reading in English or Spanish.

## Product Purpose

A digital menu: show what is available, how it looks and what it costs, fast. Success is a customer reaching a category in one tap and understanding an item (photo, price, description) in one more. The page also carries the brand promise of a small pause.

## Brand Personality

From the brand manual (Bradda, v1.0): democratic, spontaneous, joyful, consistent. Tagline: "Alegria nas pequenas pausas". The voice is light and warm, with a little humor ("Faça uma pausa, mesmo que seja espresso."). The comma is the brand symbol: a small pause.

## Anti-references

- A PDF menu or a delivery-app list.
- Dark "premium coffee" pages in black and gold. The manual asks to avoid black in large areas and very dark compositions.
- Template decoration: blurred blobs, glass panels, gradient accents, a fade on every card.
- Childish illustration. The manual's line art is minimal and timeless.

## Design Principles

1. Menu first: the category bar and the first heading are on the first screen.
2. One block, one thing. Breathing room inside each block keeps the calm.
3. Orange is a detail, never a text color.
4. Everything within thumb reach: 44px targets.
5. Motion once: no per-section entrance effects.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA. Reduced motion respected. Three languages: pt-BR (default), en, es. The menu must keep working on older phones, so nothing may depend on features newer than Safari 15.4.
